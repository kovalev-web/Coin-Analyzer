Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026. Questtick is operated by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This page explains what data we collect, why, and how to get it deleted.

What we collect
  • Account: email and password (hashed with scrypt — we never store or see your plain password), or your name/email from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile settings: avatar choice, timezone, and the daily trading limits you configure (working volume, drawdown per trade, max trades per day).
  • Binance API key (optional): only read-only keys are accepted — we reject any key with trading or withdrawal permission at save time. Stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and used solely to read your balances/fills for journal PnL.
  • Telegram chat ID (optional): set when you link our bot, used to deliver price alerts, your weekly briefing report, and journal reminders.
  • Your content: watchlist ("briefing") entries — coin symbols, dates, your notes and status — plus your trading journal entries (morning/evening check-ins, mood ratings, free-text notes, trade counts) and any price levels, alerts or trend lines you draw on charts.
  • Session data: a secure session cookie, and your IP address/browser user agent at sign-in, kept for security purposes for the life of the session (30 days).
  • Basic usage analytics: aggregated, cookie-less page performance data via Vercel Analytics/Speed Insights — we don't use this to identify you individually.
How we use it
  • To run the product: sync your watchlist, levels, alerts and journal across your devices, and show you your own trading data.
  • To generate AI analysis: when you request a coin analysis, a weekly briefing report, or a journal summary, the relevant text (coin/market data, your briefing notes, your journal entries) is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce that analysis. We do not send your email, password, or Binance/Telegram credentials to Gemini.
  • To deliver notifications you've opted into (price alerts, weekly report, journal reminders) via Telegram and in-app notifications.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use the following service providers ("sub-processors") to run Questtick. Each only receives the minimum data needed for its purpose:

  • Google — Gemini API (text you submit, for AI analysis) and Google Sign-In (if you use it).
  • Binance — only if you connect a read-only API key, to fetch your balances/fills.
  • Telegram — only if you link our bot, to deliver messages to the chat you started with it.
  • Upstash (Redis hosting) and our VPS provider — store your data as part of running the backend.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (password reset, email verification, email-change confirmation). No marketing emails.
  • Vercel — hosts the frontend and provides the anonymized analytics described above.

We do not sell or rent your data to third parties, and we don't share it for marketing purposes.

Data retention
  • Account data, journal entries, watchlist history, levels and alerts are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • In-app notifications expire automatically after 30 days.
  • Cached AI summaries are kept until the underlying data changes or you regenerate/delete them.
Your rights
  • Export: download your full journal history as a CSV file at any time.
  • Disconnect: remove your Binance API key or Telegram link at any time without deleting your whole account.
  • Delete: delete your account from Account settings → Security. This is immediate and permanent, and removes your watchlist history, journal entries, price levels/alerts/trend lines, AI summaries, trading limits, Telegram link, Binance API key, avatar and timezone setting from our servers.
  • If you're in the EU/UK or another jurisdiction with data protection law, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, or port your data — contact us below to exercise them.
Security
  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt; we never store them in plain text.
  • Binance API keys are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and we only accept read-only keys.
  • Sign-in and sign-up are rate-limited to slow down brute-force and credential-stuffing attempts.
  • Every password change and email change requires identity confirmation and notifies you, with a one-click "this wasn't me" option that revokes all active sessions.
Cookies

We use one essential session cookie required to keep you signed in — it's not used for tracking or advertising. We don't use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Age requirement

Questtick involves cryptocurrency trading information and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision; material changes will be highlighted via an in-app notice.
